## v1.0.0 — 2026-07-24

- Initial port from the standalone `Metacognition_in_Science_Explorer.html`
  artefact into the Framework Explorer (matrix view).
- 9 teaching habits (grouped by self-regulation phase: planning / monitoring /
  evaluating), 3 stages → 9 syllabus areas → 13 topics, 117 complete cells
  (`goal` / `script` / `why` / `evidence` per topic × habit).
- Data extracted directly from the artefact's own object model to guarantee a
  faithful transcription of all 117 cells.
- Stages and syllabus areas mapped to the controlled curriculum vocabulary;
  the 2017 Stage 6 syllabuses flagged `acknowledgedSuperseded` (2025 syllabuses
  roll out 2027–28).
- Framework attribution to NSW Department of Education, *Metacognition — a key
  to unlocking learning* (2020).
