# Changelog — Metacognition in Science (starter)

## v1.0.0 — 2026-07-30

- Lighter "starter" companion to the full `metacognition-nsw-science` framework,
  ported from the simpler `Science_Teaching_Metacognition_Explorer.html` artefact.
- New `grid` view type: 4 flat science contexts × 9 teaching habits = 36 cells
  (`science` / `meta` / `vignette` per cell). No habit categories and no
  citations — faithful to the lighter source (not fabricated to match the full
  matrix's `goal`/`script`/`why`/`evidence` shape).
- Contexts mapped to the controlled curriculum vocabulary (living-systems,
  working-scientifically, chem-m5).
- Intended as an on-ramp: same nine habits, four worked contexts, before the
  full 13-topic Stage 4–6 matrix.
