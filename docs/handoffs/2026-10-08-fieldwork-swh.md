## Goal

Fieldwork gains its fourth path, the Science Writing Heuristic, and Choosing a model gains "Which routine?" (ADI or
SWH?). After a shared first lesson, players choose one of two cases for lessons 2–5, Bottles (Year 10 Chemistry) or
Duckweed (Year 9 Biology), and can switch at any time. Every case opens with a card that sets the scene for someone who
hasn't read the guide, and results are shown in tables.

## Changes to existing files

### 1. `swh` guide: append worked sequence `swh-seq-s4-spinners`

"What makes a paper spinner fall slowly?" Stage 4, Forces, physics, `SC4-FOR-01`, five lessons using all seven phases,
three target conceptions (air can't push; longer wings are always slower; one drop is enough), the guide's
safety-notes pattern, reviewed by Steve Grant on 2026-10-08. The full block is in the patch. Guide `version` minor bump
(content added); `lastReviewed` 2026-10-08. No other part of the guide changes.

### 2. Game schema (`src/schema/game-lesson.ts`)

- `DataTable`: `caption`, `head` (2–5 columns), `rows` (1–8). Optional `table` on every item; the lesson refuses a row
  whose cell count differs from the headings.
- New unscored item `case`: `title`, `setting` (who, text), `lead`, `you`, optional `sofar` (phase id, when, text) and
  optional `table`.
- `track` on a lesson; `tracks` (`id`, `title`, `blurb`) on a path in `game.yaml`.

### 3. Game build (`scripts/lib/game.ts`)

- On a path with tracks, shared lessons (no `track`) come first and each track's lessons continue the numbering after
  them. Numbering is checked per track, a lesson's track must be declared, and track ids are unique.
- `case` items' `sofar` phase ids are checked against the journey guide.
- `tracks` and each lesson's `track` reach the page.

### 4. Game page (`templates/game.js`, `templates/game.html`)

- Map: after the shared lessons, a "Choose a case" group with one button per track (title, blurb, and "n of m done"
  once begun). The choice is saved in the existing progress record (`tracks`). Only the chosen case's lessons show.
- Unlocking and warm-ups stay within a case. Either case counts towards the path's mastery, so `select-4-routine`
  opens once either case reaches Explain.
- `case` card: setting, lead, table, "The lessons so far" (one row per phase in its phase colour), and "Your role". Its
  button reads "Start".
- Tables are real `<table>` markup with a caption, column headings with `scope="col"` and row headings with
  `scope="row"`. They fit 360 px.

### 5. `game.yaml`

The `swh` path (rail by stage groups, unlocks at POE Explain) with tracks `bottles` ("Bottles: Year 10 Chemistry") and
`duckweed` ("Duckweed: Year 9 Biology"). `swh` added to Choosing a model's models.

### 6. Tests (`scripts/test-game.ts`)

- New case: a fixture splits the ADI path into two cases. It checks the chooser, swapping cases, the saved choice, the
  case card and table markup, and that an undeclared track and a gap in one case's numbering are refused.
- `playLesson` chooses the right case before opening a lesson, and the lock test skips lessons in a case that isn't on
  screen.

**Version bumps:** `swh` minor. No other guide, lesson or methodology versions change.
